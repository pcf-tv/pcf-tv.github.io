School Bot

School Bot is a private tool used by a single household. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

It runs entirely on the household's own computer and does three things:

  1. Reads school-related emails from the household's own Gmail account (read-only access; it cannot send, delete, or change email).
  2. Uses an AI model that runs locally on that computer to find school events and deadlines. No email content is sent to any outside AI service.
  3. After a parent approves the list, adds reminders to a "School Reminders" Google Calendar that the tool created, so the family's phones get a reminder the evening before.
