School Bot privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

School Bot is a private tool used only by the household that runs it. It is not offered to anyone else. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Data the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only, gmail.readonly): the app reads messages in the household's own Gmail account to find school events and deadlines. It cannot send, delete, label, or modify email.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.app.created): the app creates one calendar, "School Reminders", and adds events to it. It cannot see or change any other calendar.

How the data is used

  • Email is processed only on the household's own computer, by an AI model running locally on that computer.
  • Email content is never sent to any third party, AI service, or server other than Google itself.
  • The only data the app writes back to Google is the calendar events that a parent has reviewed and approved.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and security

  • Sign-in tokens and the app's working files are stored only on the household's computer.
  • The app keeps only short summaries of detected events (title, date, and a reference to the source message) so it does not suggest the same reminder twice. Full email content is not stored by the app.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's Third-party apps & services page. Deleting the app's folder on the computer removes all locally stored data.

Compliance

School Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
